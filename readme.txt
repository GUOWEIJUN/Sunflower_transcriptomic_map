The common sunflower (Helianthus annuus L.), one of the major oilseed crops, is composed of confectionery and oilseed agronomic groups. Historically, the confectionery type is domesticated from wild populations, and further improved to the modern high-oil types. Substantial phenotypic variation exists among the confectionery and oilseed sunflowers in aspects such as flowering time (FT) and plant height (PH). However, the genetic loci and molecular mechanism underlying FT and PH in sunflower remain unclear. Here, we perform a comprehensive analysis of multi-tissue transcriptomes and whole-genome resequencing data from two cultivars, SK (confectionery) and AZB (oilseed), to identify key regulatory and hub genes.
